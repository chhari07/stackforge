**Name:** Stack

**Tagline** (60 characters max)
> Highlight anything. Stack brings it back.

**Topics:** Android · Education · Productivity · Open Source

**Description** (260 characters max)
> Stack makes you remember what you read. Save articles and PDFs from any app, highlight a line and it becomes a note, and every morning three old highlights come back. Focus timer, read-aloud, Hindi interface. Free, no ads, works offline, open source.

**Maker's first comment**
> Hey Product Hunt! 👋 I'm [Aman], a student from India.
>
> I built Stack after realising I read for hours every day (articles, PDFs, exam notes) and remembered almost none of it a week later. Read-later apps help you save. Note apps help you write. Nothing brought the important lines **back** to me.
>
> So Stack does three things:
> 1. **Save** from any app with the share sheet: links, PDFs, text
> 2. **Highlight** a line in an article, PDF or book, and it becomes a note that remembers its source
> 3. **Remember**: every morning, three old highlights come back on a spaced schedule
>
> Around that: a focus timer with music, read-aloud, EPUB, a home-screen widget, Indian news sources, and the whole app in Hindi. Optional AI can summarise or explain a line, and it always asks first.
>
> It's free, has no ads, works fully offline without an account, and is open source.
>
> I'd love to know: **what do you read that you wish you remembered better?**
