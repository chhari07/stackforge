**Full description** (4,000)

> **Stack makes you remember what you read.**
>
> You read articles, PDFs and books every day. A week later, most of it is gone. Stack fixes that in three steps:
>
> 📥 **SAVE FROM ANY APP**
> Share a link, PDF or text from Chrome, WhatsApp, Telegram or YouTube. It lands on a shelf in Stack, ready to read, even offline.
>
> ✍️ **HIGHLIGHT → NOTE**
> Select a line in an article, PDF or book. It becomes a note that remembers where it came from: the page, the article, the book.
>
> 🔁 **EVERY MORNING, 3 COME BACK**
> Stack shows you three old highlights each day. Tap "Got it" or "Show again soon", and the hard ones come back more often. Like flashcards you never had to make.
>
> **MADE FOR STUDYING**
> • PDF and EPUB reader with highlights, page notes, bookmarks and search
> • Works with any PDF: coaching notes, NCERT, PYQs, standard books
> • Import PDFs from Telegram
> • Focus sessions: a 15–60 minute timer or Pomodoro, with the PDF open and music playing, and a summary at the end
> • Streaks, reading stats and a weekly report
> • Home-screen widget with today's highlight
>
> **READ AND LISTEN**
> • Clean reader mode for any article
> • News from The Hindu, Indian Express, Mint, BBC and more, or add any RSS feed
> • Listen mode reads articles and PDFs aloud
>
> **IN YOUR LANGUAGE**
> • The whole app in English or Hindi
> • Optional AI: summarise an article, or select a hard line and tap Explain, in English or Hindi. Stack always asks before sending anything, and one switch turns AI off
>
> **YOURS, NOT OURS**
> • Free, with no ads
> • Works fully offline, with no account needed
> • Optional Google sign-in to sync across devices
> • Export notes to Markdown (Obsidian, Notion) and back up everything to one file
> • Open source: github.com/chhari07/stackforge
>
> Questions or ideas? hello@stackforge.in
