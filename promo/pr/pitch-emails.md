# Pitch emails

Short emails get answered. Write each one personally: mention something the person
actually wrote or made. Send on Tuesday to Thursday mornings, and follow up **once**
after 5 days.

## Who to send to

| Type | Examples to look up | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Indian startup press | YourStory, Inc42, The Bridge Chronicle, Entrackr | Student founder, open source, built for India |
| Tech and app sites | Android Police (apps roundups), XDA forums, 9to5Google, Android Authority | Free Pocket alternative with a review loop |
| Education press | EdexLive, India Today Education, Careers360 news | A free study tool for exam aspirants |
| Study YouTubers | Channels that review study apps or share "my study setup" | A free tool their viewers can try today |
| Newsletters | Productivity and study newsletters, open-source roundups | "One app to try this week" |

Check each name and email on the outlet's own site before sending.

## 1 · To a journalist

**Subject:** Indian student builds a free, open-source app to remember what you read

> Hi [name],
>
> I read your piece on [article] and thought this might interest you.
>
> I'm Aman, a student from [city]. I've built **Stack**, a free Android app that brings your highlights back. You save articles and PDFs from any app, highlight the lines that matter, and every morning Stack shows you three of them again so they stick. It works offline, has no ads, is open source and is available in Hindi.
>
> It opened for testing on [date]. A 35-second video: [video link]. Press kit with screenshots: [press kit link].
>
> I'd be glad to talk about why I built it or to give you early access.
>
> Thanks,
> Aman Kumar Chhari
> StackForge Labs · hello@stackforge.in
