## Descriptions

**10 words**

**50 words**
> Stack is a free, open-source Android app for students and lifelong learners. Save articles and PDFs from any app, highlight a line and it becomes a note linked to its source, and every morning three old highlights come back so they stick. It works offline, needs no account and has no ads.

**100 words**
> Most people read for hours every day and remember little of it a week later. Stack, built by StackForge Labs in India, is an Android app designed to fix that. Users save articles, PDFs and books from any app, then highlight the lines that matter. Each highlight becomes a note linked to its source, and every morning Stack brings three old highlights back on a spaced schedule. Around that loop are a focus timer with music, read-aloud, Indian news sources, a home-screen widget and a Hindi interface. Stack is free, has no ads, works fully offline without an account, and is open source.

## Main features

1. **Save from any app:** share links, PDFs and text from Chrome, WhatsApp, Telegram or YouTube
2. **Highlight → note:** select a line in an article, PDF or EPUB; it becomes a note linked to its source
3. **Daily review:** three old highlights every morning, on a spaced schedule
4. **Focus sessions:** a Pomodoro timer with the PDF open, music, and a summary at the end
5. **Read-aloud:** articles and PDFs read out, offline
6. **Explain this:** select a hard line and get a simple explanation, in English or Hindi (optional AI)
7. **Indian news:** The Hindu, Indian Express, Mint and more, all highlightable
8. **Hindi interface**, home-screen widget, Markdown export, backup and restore
